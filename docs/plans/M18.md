# M18: Mesh-independent device and communication crates

Design: docs/design.md sections 7, 9, 11, 12, 14, 16, 18, 22 and 29; also 23 and 25 for the protected paths and the standing matrix; ADR-0074 and ADR-0075
Depends on: none
Milestone issue: created with the task issues, after this plan is merged

## Scope

M18 carries out ADR-0074. It changes where items live and which error type a crate returns. It changes no kernel, no policy and no numerical result.

- **tetraloma-comm** (new; a published library crate, host-only): the communicators and what belongs to them move out of tetraloma-parallel: `CommDyn`, `Comm`, `CommExt`, `DType`, `CommScalar`, `Serial`, `Mpi`, `Neighbourhood`, `owner_hash`, `local_gpu_index` and the `mpi::ffi` wrappers. `ReduceOp` moves there from tetraloma-device. The crate gets `CommError` and its `Result`.
- **tetraloma-device:** `DeviceError` and its `Result`; `FieldScalar` beside `Real`; `ReduceOp` re-exported. `GridError`, its `Result` and `ConnKey` leave the crate.
- **tetraloma-parallel:** keeps `IndexMap` and `Scatterer`, gets `ParallelError` and its `Result`, and re-exports the public items of tetraloma-comm except `Result`.
- **tetraloma-partition:** depends on tetraloma-comm instead of tetraloma-parallel, so it is free of CubeCL, and returns `PartitionError`.
- **tetraloma-topology:** the new home of `GridError`, its `Result` and `ConnKey`, with the `From` conversions from the three foundation errors. tetraloma re-exports them, re-exports the four foundation errors at its root, and converts `PartitionError` with a crate-private helper (ADR-0075).
- **Test support below the mesh:** `tetraloma-comm-testing` (host-only) and `tetraloma-testing-base` (device), both unpublished. tetraloma-testing re-exports them, so every existing import holds.
- **tetraloma-foundation-tests** (new, unpublished; ADR-0075): primitives and the scatterer tested without a mesh crate in the build.
- **Checks:** dependency rule 9 and the two `--no-cubecl` checks in `scripts/check-workspace-deps.sh`; a new check of the public names of the four foundation crates.
- **User documentation:** tetraloma-comm joins the audited crates and the site; the book follows each move.

Unchanged for users of `tetraloma`: every path that `tetraloma` and `tetraloma::prelude` export, every `GridError` variant, every numerical result.

What does change for them: the foundation items that `tetraloma` re-exports return their own crate's error. `IndexMap` and `Scatterer` return `ParallelError`, the communicators `CommError`, the methods of `DeviceBuffer` `DeviceError`, and `Partitioner::partition` `PartitionError`. In a function that returns `tetraloma::Result`, `?` converts the first three. A function that passes such a result on without `?`, and every implementation of `Partitioner` or `CommDyn`, must change. The book has one of each (`tutorials/halo-exchange.md`, and `RoundRobin` in `tetraloma-examples/src/distributed_mesh.rs`). Such code names the errors through `tetraloma`, which re-exports the four at its root (decision 3).

Left out:

- An open set of kernel families: `KernelKind` keeps its 13 variants and `VectorPolicy::quad_tile` stays (the roadmap entry). Scatter begin and end, point-to-point messages, communicator splitting and the GPU-aware transport stay out too.
- The existing multi-rank tests of `IndexMap`, `Scatterer` and `Partitioner` in `tetraloma/tests/` (`index_map_threads.rs`, `scatter_threads.rs`, `partition_threads.rs`) stay where they are. They import through `tetraloma`, so they show that the facade's paths still hold. Only the check function of `scatter_threads.rs` moves into the test support, so that A7 and A8 call the same code (T1).
- The copies of `agree` in tetraloma-topology, tetraloma-io and tetraloma stay. They agree on `GridError` and are not foundation code.
- The `#[doc(hidden)] pub` exposure of `Strategy` and the degree-policy reader of tetraloma-geometry is M14's: tetraloma-function, which reads them, does not exist yet. They already live in tetraloma-geometry, so M18 has nothing to move.
- `UnsupportedElement` and `SpaceMismatch` (Listing F1) come with M14, in `GridError`'s new home.

M18 adds and changes no kernel. So there is no vector-size invariance test, no task has a remote run, and CUDA first sees M18's changes at the gate. The roadmap entry names no runs.

### Where the code differs from the design today

ADR-0074 edited the design ahead of the code, so the differences below are the milestone's work. Read from the code on the main branch at abb4d70.

1. **No tetraloma-comm.** The communicators are seven files of tetraloma-parallel (`comm.rs`, `counts.rs`, `graph.rs`, `hash.rs`, `local_gpu.rs`, `mpi.rs`, `serial.rs`; 1,924 lines). None of them uses a device type or CubeCL; from tetraloma-device they take only `GridError`, `Result` and `ReduceOp`. They return `GridError` (47 lines name it).
2. **`ReduceOp`** is defined in `tetraloma-device/src/primitives/mod.rs`.
3. **`GridError`** (31 variants), its `Result` and `ConnKey` are in `tetraloma-device/src/error.rs` (446 lines with their tests). tetraloma-parallel re-exports `GridError`, `ReduceOp` and `Result` from tetraloma-device; tetraloma re-exports `GridError`, `Result` and `ConnKey` from tetraloma-device too.
4. **No foundation error types.** `DeviceError`, `CommError`, `ParallelError` and `PartitionError` do not exist. `GridError` is named on 174 lines of tetraloma-device, 37 of `index_map.rs` and `scatterer.rs`, and 92 of tetraloma-partition.
5. **tetraloma-partition** depends on tetraloma-parallel and imports `CommDyn`, `CommExt`, `Serial`, `GridError` and `Result` from it. Its dev-dependency tetraloma-testing is not used by any of its code. `scripts/check-user-docs.sh --parmetis` and `ptscotch.yml` still pass `tetraloma-testing/mpi` as a dependency feature of the crate.
6. **tetraloma-io** lists tetraloma-partition as a dependency, and no source file uses it.
7. **Imports above the foundation.** About 150 lines of tetraloma-topology, tetraloma-geometry, tetraloma-io, tetraloma and tetraloma-bench name `GridError`, `Result` or `ConnKey` through `tetraloma_device::` or `tetraloma_parallel::`, and about 30 more inside multi-line imports.
8. **Two crate-private items cross the new crate boundary.**
   - `agree` (`comm.rs`) is called by `Neighbourhood::new`, which moves, and by `index_map.rs`, which stays. Its unit test in `serial.rs` passes it a `GridError::DuplicateOriginId`, a variant none of the foundation errors will have. tetraloma-partition, tetraloma-topology, tetraloma-io and tetraloma each hold a copy of their own.
   - `Neighbourhood::comm()` (`comm.rs`) is called by `scatterer.rs`. Listing S2c gives `Neighbourhood` no accessor for the communicator it holds.
9. **Test support** is all in tetraloma-testing, which depends on tetraloma: `ThreadComm` (`comm.rs`, 612 lines, importing through `tetraloma`), `comm_checks.rs` (350 lines: the communicator checks, the `IndexMap` checks and the ghost layouts in one module), `world_size.rs` (the rank-count guard), and the client functions and tolerances in `lib.rs`. `ThreadComm`'s rank budget asks tetraloma-testing's backend parser whether the test device is the CPU runtime.
10. **The checks of A6 and A7 exist already, above the foundation.**
    - `tetraloma/tests/scatter_threads.rs` checks `forward` and `reverse` with Add, Min, Max and Insert against a host reference, on the layouts of `ghost_layout_sized`, for the four scalar types and block sizes 1 and 3.
    - `tetraloma-device/tests/primitives_large.rs` checks the primitives of A6 at 10^6 and 10^7 elements, with the host references and comparison helpers of `tests/common/mod.rs` (485 lines, shared by six test files).
11. **Dev-dependencies through the facade.** tetraloma-device and tetraloma-parallel dev-depend on tetraloma-testing: 97 lines of tetraloma-device's examples and tests and 9 lines of tetraloma-parallel's examples name `tetraloma_testing::`. The doctest runs of `scripts/check-docs.sh`, `scripts/check-user-docs.sh`, `ci.yml` and `ptscotch.yml` pass `tetraloma-testing/mpi` as a dependency feature of those crates. `docs/dev-environment.md`, the hpc-test skill and the panic message of `test_client` name `--features tetraloma-testing/<backend>` for every test run.
12. **`FieldScalar`** does not exist, and `num-complex` is not among the workspace dependencies.
13. **The documentation audit** lists nine crates in `scripts/doc_audit.py`, `scripts/check-user-docs.sh` and the `docs` job of `ci.yml`; `scripts/build-site.sh` takes its list from `doc_audit.py`. M11's task T4 (#72, open) adds tetraloma-element to the same lists. Whichever of the two tasks lands second merges the lists; the counts in comments follow the lists.
14. **`scripts/check-workspace-deps.sh`** takes one `--no-cubecl` crate and has no check of rule 9.
15. **The foundation-only test crate** of the roadmap entry had no name and no row in the crate table of section 18. ADR-0075 names it tetraloma-foundation-tests (decision 1).
16. **MPI tests** live in tetraloma-mpi-tests, which depends on tetraloma (section 22, "MPI tests"). The roadmap entry asks for "the same scatter tests" of the foundation-only crate under MPICH and Open MPI. ADR-0075 puts the shared checks into tetraloma-testing-base and the MPI target into tetraloma-mpi-tests (decision 2).
17. **The facade does not export the new error types.** Section 18 has tetraloma re-export `GridError`, `Result` and `ConnKey` and convert `PartitionError`; it lists no re-export of `CommError`, `DeviceError`, `ParallelError` or `PartitionError`. tetraloma-examples and tetraloma-testing implement `Partitioner` and `CommDyn` with the names they get from `tetraloma`, and depend on no foundation crate. ADR-0075 adds the re-exports (decision 3).

Carried over from earlier milestones, and still true:

- Enum variants cannot be stubbed, so a T0 declares them whole (M6, T0).
- The checks shared by the thread-rank tests and the MPI targets are library functions of a test-support crate; the MPI target only starts MPI and calls them (M6, T0 and T3).
- `ThreadComm` implements `CommDyn::raw`, which exists only under `mpi`. A crate that holds `ThreadComm` needs an `mpi` feature that is on whenever the communicator crate's is (M6, T0).
- `IndexMap::send_lists` and `recv_lists` are hidden test accessors (M6, T5; ADR-0028). They stay in tetraloma-parallel.

### Hidden items (dependency rule 7)

M18 adds two permanent hidden items, both in tetraloma-comm and both documented as not part of the stable API:

- `Neighbourhood::comm(&self) -> Option<&Rc<dyn CommDyn>>` becomes `#[doc(hidden)] pub`, for `Scatterer` in tetraloma-parallel, which exchanges through the communicator its `Neighbourhood` holds (section 11). Its named test is `neighbourhood_holds_its_parent_communicator`, a unit test of tetraloma-comm (T2).
- `agree<T, E: From<CommError>>(comm: &dyn CommDyn, local: Result<T, E>) -> Result<T, E>` becomes `#[doc(hidden)] pub`, for `IndexMap` in tetraloma-parallel and for tetraloma-partition, whose own copy goes (T3). It is generic over the error, so each caller agrees on its own error type. Its named test is the existing `agreed_errors_on_one_rank`, which moves with it and uses a `CommError` variant (T2).

The stubs of T0 are hidden until the task that implements them removes the attribute. No stub remains hidden after T8.

How the rows are read:

- **A1.** The script gains a list of crates whose normal and build dependencies must hold no mesh crate of rule 9, and prints the list in its summary. `--no-mesh CRATE` adds a crate to it. The negative control is `scripts/check-workspace-deps.sh --no-mesh tetraloma-topology`, which must exit with status 3: tetraloma-topology depends on tetraloma-dims. The option exists from T0; the row is met when T2 puts the four foundation crates on the default list.
- **A2.** `--no-cubecl` becomes repeatable. From T3 on, the default run also checks both crates, over normal, build and dev dependencies as it does for tetraloma-element.
- **A3.** The check builds the API pages of the four crates and reads the names of their structs, enums, unions, traits, type aliases, functions, methods, constants and statics. Enum variants are not checked (ADR-0074: `KernelKind` keeps `DofTransform`). Hidden items are not on the pages.
  - A name in camel case fails if it contains one of the five words as the roadmap writes them. `Connectivity` would fail; `Connect` would too, which the roadmap's wording implies.
  - A name in snake or upper case fails if one of its segments, without regard to case, is one of the five words or its plural. `conn_key` and `MESH_PAD` would fail; `connect` and `reconnect` would pass. The roadmap's capitalised words do not say how such names are read; this reading is part of the sign-off on the new script.
  - Methods are checked with the functions.
  - At T0 the check fails on `GridError` and `ConnKey`, the only two matches today. The negative control is `scripts/check-foundation-names.sh --crates tetraloma-topology`, which must exit with status 3. `scripts/check-user-docs.sh` runs the same check on the site's pages, which are built with the site's features.
- **A4.** The test builds each of the 25 variants (5 of `CommError`, 5 of `DeviceError`, 8 of `ParallelError`, 7 of `PartitionError`) with distinct field values, converts it, and matches the `GridError` variant and its fields. The four enums are `#[non_exhaustive]`, so a test outside their crates cannot prove that its list is complete. Each defining crate therefore has a unit test with a match over its own enum without a wildcard arm: a new variant breaks that crate's build and points to this test. The test is a unit test of tetraloma because the conversion helper is crate-private (decision 3).
- **A5.** "Before the milestone" is the main branch at abb4d70, where the file's blob is `1663327c`. No task may touch the file, so the command passes from T0 on; every task and the gate run it. The existing check of the protected path (`cargo test -p tetraloma-device --test integration policy::`) runs as before.
- **A6, A7.** Both tests call check functions of the test support, the same code that `primitives_large.rs` and `scatter_threads.rs` call (T1). They pass from T1 on and are not ignored: they exercise the paths that T2–T6 move. What is missing until T7 is the dependency clause of A6, which `scripts/check-workspace-deps.sh` checks: the normal dependency tree of the foundation-only crate holds no workspace crate other than the five. Sizes for A6: 10^6 elements for every primitive and every one of the four scalar types; radix sort on u32 keys with a u32 payload. A7: ghost layouts from three seeds on each of 1–4 ranks. On a device without f64, `forward` runs in f64 (it moves u32 words) and `reverse` must return `UnsupportedPrecision`, as the check asserts today; the check names the variant of the error type `Scatterer` returns at the time (T5).
- **A8** runs the check function of A7 with `Mpi` as the communicator. It runs from T1 on, like A7.
- **A11** has no test of its own. The gate runs the milestone tier; "unchanged" is read as: no test, tolerance or check of the standing matrix was changed to make it pass, which the gate's record pass reads from the merged diffs (decision 5).

## Tasks

The check commands of `AGENTS.md` stay as they are. The scripts and workflows behind them gain what ADR-0074 ("Changes if accepted", Interfaces and tests; Process documents and scripts) asks for. The sections of T0–T3, T6 and T7 name the files and give those tasks that job (rule 3). One command changes for developers: a test run of foundation crates alone on a GPU backend names `tetraloma-testing-base/<backend>` from T7 on (T7).

T1 and T2 need ADR-0075 on the main branch: T1 for the new crate and the home of the checks, T2 for the re-exports of the error types.

**Re-exports.** tetraloma re-exports each foundation error at its root, not in the prelude, from the task that makes the crate return it: `CommError` in T2, `PartitionError` in T3, `DeviceError` in T4 and `ParallelError` in T5 (decision 3). Code above the facade names the errors through `tetraloma`.

**Stubs.** A move has no interface to stub, so T0 and T1 use three kinds:

- *Error enums* are declared whole and `#[doc(hidden)]` (variants cannot be stubbed), without `Display` and `std::error::Error`. Their `From` conversions into `GridError` and tetraloma's helper have the body `unimplemented!("pending M18.T<k>")`.
- *`FieldScalar`* is declared hidden with its four implementations and `PARTS = 0`, the stand-in for `unimplemented!` in a constant.
- *The two test-support crates* start as shims that re-export from tetraloma-testing (T1). T7 turns the dependency round. The tests of A6–A8 are final from T1 on and pass through the shims.

**Order.** Each of T2–T6 leaves the workspace building and every existing test passing. `GridError` stays in tetraloma-device until T6, with temporary `From` conversions next to it, and `tetraloma_device::Result` and `tetraloma_parallel::Result` stay its alias until T6. So the mesh crates keep compiling while the foundation crates change their error types one at a time, and T6 changes each import line of the mesh crates once.
