## Scope

M13 delivers high-order quadrature rules, symmetric simplex tables, sub-entity and vertex-permutation point maps, facet-rule matching permutations, custom point sets with `Element` tabulation, and per-cell reference point containers (Listing Q1, section 27).

- **tetraloma-refcell:**
  - `QuadratureType` (`Default`, `Gauss`, `Symmetric`, `GaussLobatto`) and `quadrature_rule`, backed by committed compact-orbit symmetric tables in `tetraloma-refcell/tables/` with provenance and licence records in `tetraloma-refcell/tables/sources.json` (degrees 1–30 on triangles and tetrahedra; Worku–Hicken–Zingg, `OptimalDesignLab/SummationByParts.jl`, MIT License; ADR-0081), `Gauss` (degrees 0–60 on all eight cells) and `GaussLobatto` (degrees 0–79, `n = 2..=41` points per direction on intervals, quadrilaterals and hexahedra).
  - `sub_entity_points_to_cell` (generalising `facet_points_to_cell` to every sub-entity dimension), `rule_permutation` (A.6 reorientation permutation of symmetric facet rules) and `permute_vertices` (affine reference-point mapping under cell vertex symmetries).
  - `RefCellError` becomes `#[non_exhaustive]` and gains the Listing Q1 error variants (ADR-0081).
- **tetraloma-topology:** `GridError` gains `UnsupportedQuadratureType`, `UnsupportedDerivativeOrder` and `CellMismatch` (ADR-0081).
- **tetraloma-element:** replaces its private `sub_entity_points_to_cell` in `src/transform.rs` with `tetraloma_refcell::sub_entity_points_to_cell` (M11 gate review finding G12), and gains acceptance test A5 (`gll_mass_matrix_is_diagonal`).
- **tetraloma-geometry:** gains `tetraloma-element` as a normal dependency (section 18), re-exports `QuadratureType`, and adds `QuadRule::new`, `FacetQuadRule::new`, `PointSet::from_points`, `PointSet::tabulate_element` and `CellPoints` (`new`, `block`, `len`, `is_empty`, `cells`, `points`, `client`; ADR-0081). `PointSet::len()` counts points from `points.len() / TD::N` so weightless point sets work cleanly, and `TensorFactors` extends to `GaussLobatto` rules and `Default`/`Symmetric` triangular facets.
- **tetraloma:** re-exports `QuadratureType` and `CellPoints` at the root and in `prelude` (ADR-0081).
- **tetraloma-testing and tetraloma-mpi-tests:** shared interior-facet point-matching check (A6) and MPI target `facet_rule_permutation`.
- **User documentation:** rustdoc with book links and running `# Example` doctests on every new or changed public item, and updates to `docs/user/src/concepts/quadrature.md` and `docs/user/src/tutorials/quadrature.md`.

Left out:

- Singular and near-singular quadrature (Sauter–Schwab, Duffy); adaptive and nested rules; symmetric rules on prisms and pyramids; rules on curved sub-entities (the roadmap entry and section 27, "Out of scope").
- Device evaluation of grid functions at `CellPoints` (`Function::evaluate_at`, M15) and the `#[doc(hidden)] pub` exposure of `Strategy`, the degree-policy reader and the device basis evaluator for `tetraloma-function` (M14/M15, when `tetraloma-function` exists to consume them).
- N1curl and RT elements (M12, planned in parallel). `PointSet::tabulate_element` is generic over any `Element`, so M13 tests all M11 elements (and M12's if M12 is merged first); if M13 merges before M12, M12 extends A7 to N1curl and RT (ADR-0081).
- Cross-crate deduplication of `tetraloma-geometry/src/sumfact/mod.rs`'s `permutation` and `tetraloma-refcell::LagrangeElement` (M11 gate review G12): left untouched to keep hot kernel-table construction and `LagrangeElement`'s public API stable.

M13 adds and changes no CubeCL kernel. So there is no vector-size invariance test, no task has a remote run, and CUDA first sees M13's changes at the gate.

### Where the code differs from the design today

Read from the code on the main branch at `2ffa13f`.

1. **No `QuadratureType`, `quadrature_rule` or stored tables in `tetraloma-refcell`.** Only `gauss_rule` exists (`tetraloma-refcell/src/quadrature.rs`), with no degree cap and no `Result`. `tetraloma-refcell/tables/` does not exist.
2. **Symmetric tetrahedral tables.** Section 27's table names "(Xiao–Gimbutas), degree 1–30". Verified in the pinned `fenics-basix==0.11.0` (MIT): `xiao_gimbutas` on triangles covers degrees 1–30 and is fully symmetric (`max_pt_err = 1.63e-16`), but on tetrahedra it stops at degree 15 and is **asymmetric** (only degrees 1 and 5 are invariant under vertex permutations, failing A3). `modepy`'s Jaśkowiec–Sukumar data is CC BY 4.0. `OptimalDesignLab/SummationByParts.jl` (Worku, Hicken & Zingg; commit `9d2a6e7aa8a34d18e568a60052870307ac9d6321`, MIT License) provides compact-orbit, exact-symmetry, positive-interior rules for both triangles (1–84) and tetrahedra (1–40), with strictly fewer points than `Gauss` for every degree 4–30 (ADR-0081).
3. **`RefCellError` and `GridError` variants.** Section 27 says `quadrature_rule` returns `RefCellError::UnsupportedNodeFamily` for an unsupported rule type, but `RefCellError::UnsupportedNodeFamily` (`tetraloma-refcell/src/error.rs:31`) carries `family: NodeFamily` and cannot hold `QuadratureType`. `RefCellError` is exhaustive (no crate outside `tetraloma-refcell` matches it exhaustively) and has no variants for invalid sub-entities, orientation bits, unsymmetric rules, vertex permutations or cell mismatches. `GridError` (`tetraloma-topology/src/error.rs:87`) lacks `UnsupportedQuadratureType`, `UnsupportedDerivativeOrder` and `CellMismatch` (ADR-0081).
4. **Private sub-entity map in `tetraloma-element`.** `tetraloma-element/src/transform.rs:64-83` holds a private `sub_entity_points_to_cell` marked as an M13 follow-up (M11.T6; M11 gate review finding G12). T3 replaces it with `tetraloma_refcell::sub_entity_points_to_cell`.
5. **`tetraloma-geometry` does not depend on `tetraloma-element`.** Section 18 lists `element` among `tetraloma-geometry`'s dependencies; `tetraloma-geometry/Cargo.toml` does not list it yet. T0 adds it.
6. **`PointSet::len()` assumes non-empty weights.** `tetraloma-geometry/src/point_set.rs:188-190` returns `self.weights.len()`, which breaks when `PointSet::from_points` accepts empty `weights`. `tetraloma-geometry/src/measure.rs:149` checks `pts.is_empty()` without checking `pts.weights().is_empty()`. T5 fixes both (ADR-0081).
7. **`TensorFactors` hardcodes `gauss_rule`.** `tetraloma-geometry/src/rule.rs:91-99` and `tetraloma-geometry/src/sumfact/mod.rs:130-231` build `TensorFactors` from `gauss_rule` directly. T4 generalises them to take the 1D/facet point arrays from `quadrature_rule`, so sum factorisation applies to `GaussLobatto` rules on quadrilaterals/hexahedra and `Default`/`Symmetric` triangular facets on prisms.
8. **Missing Listing Q1 items and re-exports.** `sub_entity_points_to_cell`, `rule_permutation`, `permute_vertices`, `QuadRule::new`, `FacetQuadRule::new`, `PointSet::from_points`, `PointSet::tabulate_element` and `CellPoints` do not exist. `QuadratureType` and `CellPoints` are not re-exported by `tetraloma-geometry`, `tetraloma` or `tetraloma::prelude` (ADR-0081).
9. **Parallel M12/M13 scope of A7.** `docs/roadmap.md` gives M13 `Depends on: M11` while M12 is planned in parallel; ADR-0081 clarifies A7 so neither milestone blocks the other.

Carried over from earlier milestones, and still true:

- `tetraloma-refcell` and `tetraloma-element` are built with `opt-level = 3` in the dev profile (`Cargo.toml:96-102`).
- `tetraloma-refcell` returns `RefCellError`, which `tetraloma-geometry` maps to `GridError` (`tetraloma-geometry/src/names.rs`).
- Shared checks between thread-rank tests and `tetraloma-mpi-tests` live in `tetraloma-testing`; the MPI target starts MPI via `tetraloma_mpi_tests::run` and calls the check (M6, M18).
- Enum variants cannot be stubbed, so T0 declares them whole (M6, M18).

How the rows are read:

- **A1.** Orthonormal basis functions satisfy `∫ ψ_0 = √|K̂|` and `∫ ψ_k = 0` for `k > 0`. Relative error is `|approx − √|K̂|| / √|K̂| ≤ 10^−14` for `k = 0` and `|approx| / √|K̂| ≤ 10^−14` for `k > 0`. `GaussLobatto` tests `n = 2..=41` points per direction via `degree = 2n − 3` (`degree` in `1..=79`, odd degrees, plus `degree = 0` giving `n = 2`). On non-tensor cells (`Triangle`, `Tetrahedron`, `Prism`, `Pyramid`), `polyset::tabulate` is summed in point chunks so peak memory stays modest; on tensor cells (`Quadrilateral`, `Hexahedron`), where `n = 41` has `68,921` points and `512,000` 3D basis functions, the test verifies that the returned 2D/3D `QuadratureRule` is the exact tensor product of the 1D rule (bitwise on coordinates and within `10^−15` on weights), integrates the full 1D `polyset` up to `degree = 79` to `10^−14` (equivalent by Fubini's theorem), and directly integrates the 2D/3D `polyset` over lower degrees (`Quadrilateral` up to `degree = 20`, `Hexahedron` up to `degree = 10`).
- **A2.** Checks `Gauss` for degrees 0–60 on all eight cells (`Point` has empty coordinates and weight `1.0 > 0`), `Symmetric` for degrees 1–30 on triangles and tetrahedra, and `Default` for degrees 0–60 on all eight cells. Unit tests also check that `GaussLobatto` weights are positive and points lie in `[0, 1]^tdim` with endpoints on the boundary.
- **A3.** Checks all 6 permutations of the reference triangle and all 24 permutations of the reference tetrahedron across degrees 1–30: under each affine vertex permutation, every quadrature point maps to a unique point of the rule within `10^−15` (max norm) with matching weight within `10^−15`.
- **A4.** Checks `quadrature_rule(cell, d, Default)?.weights.len() < quadrature_rule(cell, d, Gauss)?.weights.len()` for every `d` in `4..=30` on `Triangle` and `Tetrahedron`.
- **A5.** For `n = 2..=17`, constructs `Element` for `(Lagrange, cell, n - 1, Nodal(Gll))` and `quadrature_rule(cell, 2 * n - 3, GaussLobatto)` on `Interval`, `Quadrilateral` and `Hexahedron`. Computes the full dense mass matrix `M = Φᵀ diag(w) Φ` directly on `Interval` (`n = 2..=17`), `Quadrilateral` (`n = 2..=17`) and `Hexahedron` (`n = 2..=8`), checking every off-diagonal entry `|M_{ij}| ≤ 10^−14` (relative to `max(M_{ii}, M_{jj})` and absolute) and every diagonal entry matches its nodal quadrature weight to `10^−14`. On `Hexahedron` `n = 9..=17` (up to `N = 4,913` nodes), evaluates `Φ` at the `n³` rule points, checks diagonal entries directly, and verifies the rigorous `O(N²)` Cauchy–Schwarz bound `|M_{ij}| ≤ |w_{q(i)} φ_i(x_{q(i)}) φ_j(x_{q(i)})| + |w_{q(j)} φ_i(x_{q(j)}) φ_j(x_{q(j)})| + r_i r_j ≤ 10^−14` (where `r_i = (Σ_{q ≠ q(i)} w_q φ_i(x_q)²)^{1/2}`) over all `i ≠ j`.
- **A6.** Uses `GhostMode::SharedFacet`, `FacetQuadRule::new(..., QuadratureType::Default)` at degrees 3 and 5 (covering symmetric triangular facets, Gauss quadrilateral facets and Gauss interval facets), and randomly permuted vertex orderings on a tetrahedral box, a hexahedral box, the mixed box (`tetraloma_testing::mixed_box`) and a triangulated sphere (`D2` in `D3`), checking `physical_points_block` mapped through `rule_permutation` from both cells of every owned interior facet in `f32` (`10^−5`) and, where supported, `f64` (`10^−12`). The check lives in `tetraloma-testing`, called by the MPI target `facet_rule_permutation` (1–4 ranks) and by a `ThreadComm` integration test in `tetraloma/tests/`.
- **A7.** Runs in `f32` (`10^−5` relative) and, where supported, `f64` (`10^−12` relative), for `nderivs = 0` and `1`, on both built-in `QuadRule` point sets and `PointSet::from_points` sets, checking layout `[derivative][q][k][component]` and caching against `Element::tabulate` for every supported element with `p ≤ 6` (all M11 Lagrange elements across all cells and variants, plus M12 N1curl and RT if M12 is merged; ADR-0081).
- **A8.** Runs on every cell type of dimension 1–3 (`Interval`, `Triangle`, `Quadrilateral`, `Tetrahedron`, `Hexahedron`, `Prism`, `Pyramid`) at geometry degrees 1, 2 and 3 (curved physical cells with non-affine node perturbations), comparing `physical_points` of a cell whose vertices (and corresponding Lagrange nodes) are permuted by cell symmetries against `physical_points` of the original cell evaluated at `permute_vertices`-mapped points, to `10^−12` in `f64` (and `10^−5` in `f32` where `f64` is unavailable). `CellType::Point` is covered by unit tests in `tetraloma-refcell`.
- **A9.** Uses the exact test module path `quadrature_tables::` required by section 23 row 9 (`cargo test -p tetraloma-refcell --test integration quadrature_tables::`). Verifies every data file under `tetraloma-refcell/tables/` (excluding `sources.json`) has an entry in `tables/sources.json` naming its upstream source, commit/version, MIT/Apache-2.0-compatible SPDX licence and matching SHA-256 checksum, and that `sources.json` names no missing file.

### T2: `QuadratureType` and `quadrature_rule`

Implements `QuadratureType` and `quadrature_rule` in `tetraloma-refcell` for all four rule types (`Default`, `Gauss`, `Symmetric`, `GaussLobatto`) and removes their `#[doc(hidden)]` attributes. Enables A1, A2, A3, A4 and A5.

- `Symmetric`: embeds `tetraloma-refcell/tables/` (compact orbits) at compile time (`include_str!`), expands the symmetry orbits (`S1` → 1, `S21` → 3, `S111` → 6 on triangles; `S1` → 1, `S31` → 4, `S22` → 6, `S211` → 12, `S1111` → 24 on tetrahedra) in deterministic order, scales weights by the reference cell volume (`1/2` triangle, `1/6` tetrahedron), and enforces `degree` in `1..=30` (`UnsupportedDegree` outside; `UnsupportedQuadratureType` on other cells).
- `Gauss`: delegates to `gauss_rule` for `degree` in `0..=60` on all eight cells (`UnsupportedDegree` above 60).
- `GaussLobatto`: on `Interval`, `Quadrilateral` and `Hexahedron`, computes `n = (degree + 3).div_ceil(2)` (`degree` in `0..=79` → `n` in `2..=41`, `UnsupportedDegree` above 79; `UnsupportedQuadratureType` on other cells), uses `jacobi::gll_points(n)` and exact weights `w_i = 1 / (n (n − 1) P_{n−1}(2 t_i − 1)²)`, and forms the tensor product with first coordinate fastest.
- `Default`: `Symmetric` for `1..=30` and `Gauss` for `0` and `31..=60` on triangles and tetrahedra; `Gauss` for `0..=60` on all other cells (including `Point`).
- Unit tests: every error case (`UnsupportedDegree`, `UnsupportedQuadratureType`), `Point` cell, `GaussLobatto` boundary inclusion and weight positivity, determinism.
- Documentation: rustdoc with book links and running `# Example` doctests for `QuadratureType`, `quadrature_rule` and `RefCellError`'s new variants; updates `docs/user/src/concepts/quadrature.md` (`Quadrature rules`).

### T3: `sub_entity_points_to_cell`, `permute_vertices` and `rule_permutation`

Implements the three host point-mapping and permutation functions of Listing Q1 in `tetraloma-refcell`, removes their `#[doc(hidden)]` attributes, and resolves M11 gate review finding G12. Enables A8.

- `sub_entity_points_to_cell(cell, d, e, points)`: validates `d <= tdim(cell)`, `e < num_sub_entities(cell, d)` (else `InvalidSubEntity`), and point-array shape (`PointsShape`, with `d == 0` requiring empty `points` and returning the vertex coordinates), then applies the affine sub-entity map `ξ = v_0 + Σ_k t_k (v_{k+1} − v_0)`. `facet_points_to_cell` delegates to it (keeping its existing panic contract on invalid inputs).
- `tetraloma-element/src/transform.rs`: replaces its private `sub_entity_points_to_cell` helper (lines 64–83) with `tetraloma_refcell::sub_entity_points_to_cell` (M11 gate review G12).
- `permute_vertices(cell, perm, points)`: validates `perm` is a combinatorial symmetry (vertex-edge graph automorphism) of `cell` (else `InvalidVertexPermutation`) and validates `points.len()` (`PointsShape`), then applies the exact affine map `Φ(v̂_k) = v̂_{perm[k]}` across all eight cell types (avoiding any `1/(1 − z)` division at the pyramid apex).
- `rule_permutation(entity, rule, bits)`: validates `entity` is `Point`, `Interval`, `Triangle` or `Quadrilateral` and `bits` is a valid A.6 bit field for `entity` (else `InvalidOrientationBits`), validates `rule.cell == entity` (else `CellMismatch`), applies the A.6 reorientation map `Φ_bits` (reflection first, then `rot` rotations, matching M11's `transform.rs` and ADR-0077) to `rule.points`, matches each mapped point to a unique point of `rule` within `10^−12` with matching weight within `10^−12` (else `UnsymmetricRule`), and returns the gather permutation `Vec<u32>`.
- Unit and property tests: every sub-entity of every cell, every symmetry group order (`1, 2, 6, 8, 24, 48, 12, 8`), group composition `permute_vertices(p ∘ q) = permute_vertices(p) ∘ permute_vertices(q)`, `rule_permutation` on `Gauss`, `Symmetric` and `GaussLobatto` rules, rejection of collapsed Gauss on triangles (`UnsymmetricRule`), and all error variants.
- Enables A8 (`tetraloma` `permute_vertices::matches_physical_points_of_permuted_cell`).
- Documentation: rustdoc with book links and `# Example` doctests for the three functions; updates `docs/user/src/concepts/quadrature.md`.

### T5: `PointSet::from_points`, `PointSet::tabulate_element` and `CellPoints`

- `PointSet` (`tetraloma-geometry/src/point_set.rs`):

  - Implements `PointSet::from_points(client, cell, points, weights)`: checks precision, validates `points.len().is_multiple_of(TD::N)` and `weights.is_empty() || weights.len() == num_points` (else `GridError::LengthMismatch`), uploads narrowed weights (empty `DeviceBuffer` when `weights` is empty), and shares the same tabulation cache as built-in rules.
